Privacy policy

Version 2026-11 · Last updated: [date]

Parental Guardian is a family safety service. A parent installs an app on their child's Android phone and sees the phone's location, app usage and battery level in their own app or web dashboard. This policy explains exactly what is collected and why.

1. Who is responsible

[Your name or company], [address], [country] ("we"). Contact: [contact email].

2. What we collect

DataFromWhy
Name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), when you accepted this policyThe parentTo run your account and prove consent
Children's first names and the names you give places (for example "Home")The parentTo show the data and send alerts
Location (latitude, longitude, accuracy, time), about every 5 minutes, including when the app is closedThe child's phoneTo show where the child is and to send arrive/leave alerts

We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, photos, files, browsing history, keystrokes, the microphone or the camera.

3. Consent and children

The parent app is for adults. The child component is installed and set up by a parent or legal guardian, who confirms when creating an account that they are the parent or legal guardian of the children they add. The child phone shows a clear disclosure screen before anything is shared and a notification is shown whenever the app is active. We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we never sell it.

4. How long we keep it

Location, battery and app usage data is deleted automatically after 30 days. Account data is kept until you delete your account.

5. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the service, under their own privacy terms: hosting and database ([hosting provider, region]), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Cloudflare (website hosting), and OpenStreetMap (map images; the parent's device contacts their servers when a map is shown). [If you enable error reporting: Sentry receives technical error reports that do not include location or usage data.] We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

6. Where data is stored

Our servers are located in [country/region]. Data may therefore be processed outside the country where you live. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protect access to the database.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

You can delete your account and all data at any time: in the parent app (Account, Delete my account) or on the website (Delete account). Disconnecting a phone stops collection from it immediately. You can ask for a copy of your data, or for correction, by writing to [contact email]. If you uninstall the child app, collection from that phone stops.

8. Security

Passwords and device tokens are stored hashed. Every parent can only access their own children's data. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you we will tell you and the authorities as required by law.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters we will update the version above and ask you to accept it again.

10. Complaints

Contact us first at [contact email]. You may also contact your national data protection authority.
